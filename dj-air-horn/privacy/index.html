DJ Air Horn Privacy Policy

A privacy policy for the DJ Air Horn Android app

Effective date: 30 September 2026

I’m Francis Auwerda, the developer of DJ Air Horn, a free Android app available on Google Play. This policy explains what information may be processed when you use the app.

What the app does

DJ Air Horn plays sound effects stored and played locally on your device. You do not need an account to use it, and the app does not collect information from you directly for an account or profile.

Advertising

DJ Air Horn displays banner advertisements provided by Google AdMob. To serve ads, measure ad performance, and help detect fraud or abuse, Google and its advertising partners may process information such as your Advertising ID, device identifiers, IP address, approximate location, and app or ad usage data. The information and choices available to you can depend on your device, region, and Google’s policies.

For more information, see Google’s Privacy Policy and Google AdMob’s information about how it uses data.

Google Play Services

The app may use Google Play Services, including services needed to provide advertising. Google handles information collected by those services under its own policies.

Children’s privacy

DJ Air Horn is not directed at children under 13. I do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child has provided personal information in connection with the app, please contact me so I can look into it.

Changes to this policy

I may update this policy when the app or its services change. The updated policy will be posted on this page with a new effective date.

Contact

If you have a privacy question about DJ Air Horn, email me at fauwerda@gmail.com. My developer website is francisauwerda.com.
